## Description

The book ends with homework:
[Chapter 15](../../chapters/15-partners-and-caregivers/index.md)'s two
capstone documents, written by each reader for the other. This builder walks
you through them section by section. Choose your role — **I have ADHD**
(the eight-section Personal Owner's Manual) or **I'm the family** (the
six-section Family Support Plan) — and each section presents a prompt card:
its purpose, two or three starter questions, an example line from the book's
running characters, and the chapters it draws on. The blank-page barrier gets
the Chapter 12 treatment; the words remain entirely yours.

Drafts save automatically to your browser's local storage — nothing leaves
your device. **Assemble** produces the full document with a date stamp, review
date line, and — honestly — any sections still empty, marked rather than
hidden. Print it, then follow the exchange instructions: a real date, dinner
attached, read all of it before responding, and the masking and our-lines
sections get read twice.

## Lesson Plan

1. **Draft** (15+ min, often across days): Each reader completes their document. Out of order is fine; the rail tracks progress.
2. **Assemble and print** (2 min): Empty sections stay visible — they're the honest to-do list.
3. **Exchange** (the point): A scheduled dinner. Two questions before any edit.
4. **Calendar** (1 min): The annual review date goes on the shared calendar before the table is cleared.
