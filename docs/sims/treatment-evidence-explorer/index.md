## Description

[Chapter 8](../../chapters/08-treatment-beyond-medication/index.md) ranks the
treatment landscape by where each option's evidence lives on the pyramid —
from testimonials at the floor to meta-analyses at the summit. This chart
makes the ranking explorable: bar length is evidence strength, color depth is
approximate effect magnitude, hover gives the one-line verdict, and clicking a
bar opens the full reasoning. Both scales are deliberately qualitative — false
precision is its own kind of misinformation.

The **claim tester** deals six marketplace claims — the five-star supplement,
the clinic's internal neurofeedback data, the honestly boring omega-3
sentence — and asks you to place each on its pyramid floor, with explained
feedback. The skill being trained: asking *which floor does this claim live
on* before spending money, hours, or hope.

## Lesson Plan

1. **Read the shape** (3 min): Which cheap, strong-evidence rows surprise people? (Sleep and parent training, usually.)
2. **Verdicts** (4 min): Click neurofeedback and supplements. Discuss the "better controls, smaller effect" tell.
3. **Test** (5 min): Run all six claims. Note that the trustworthy claim sounds the most boring.
4. **Audit** (3 min): Each family lists what they currently spend on, and its floor.
