## Description

[Chapter 7](../../chapters/07-medication/index.md) claims that structured
tracking converts anecdotes into titration signals. This tracker proves it on
Dev's eight weeks: three target symptoms (homework starts, calm mornings,
teacher reports) and a side-effect burden line, charted weekly, with every
dose step annotated. Hover any week to read the diary note behind the numbers
— including week 3's untouched lunch and week 7's flat-at-dinner overshoot.

**Decision mode** pauses at weeks 3, 5, and 7 and asks you to make the call:
hold, step, engineer around a side effect, or step back. The feedback names
the data feature that drives each answer — real target response plus a common
side effect reads differently than flatness with no target gain. The exercise
is about reading data; every real dose decision belongs to the prescriber, and
the sim says so.

## Lesson Plan

1. **Read** (3 min): Hover weeks 0-8 in order, reading the diary aloud.
2. **Decide** (5 min): Make all three calls before reading feedback. Compare answers in pairs.
3. **Transfer** (4 min): Have each family draft their own three target symptoms — concrete, observable, theirs — for a real tracking sheet.
4. **Boundary** (1 min): Repeat the rule: the sheet informs the prescriber; it never replaces them.
