## Description

[Chapter 6](../../chapters/06-co-occurring-conditions/index.md) explains that
co-occurring conditions crowd around ADHD through three mechanisms — shared
biological roots, consequences of the unexplained years, and mutual
entanglement — plus a fourth relationship that is really about differential
diagnosis (the look-alikes). This map encodes each mechanism as an edge type.
Click any condition for its field-guide entry: definition, relationship, the
tell that should prompt screening, and its treatment lane.

The filter buttons isolate one mechanism class at a time — filtering to
*consequences* shows what the untreated years manufacture; filtering to
*entangled* shows what must be treated in parallel. The **Dev, 15** button
loads the chapter's case: ADHD plus a reading disability plus emerging low
mood, with the daily-defeat edge drawn in — the picture that explains why
"raise the dose" was the wrong dial.

## Lesson Plan

1. **Tour** (4 min): Click through all twelve conditions; note each tell.
2. **Mechanisms** (4 min): Use the filters. Ask: which class responds to treating the ADHD itself, and which needs its own lane?
3. **Case** (3 min): Load Dev. Trace why the stimulant dial couldn't fix 10th grade.
4. **Screen** (3 min): Each learner privately marks any tell that rings true — that list is the agenda for the next clinician visit.
