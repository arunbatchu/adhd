## Description

[Chapter 14](../../chapters/14-family-life/index.md)'s conflict loop, drawn as
a ring you can operate: demand at a bad moment → disappointing response →
escalated repetition → RSD spike → blowup → ledgers updated → ambient tension
rises → and back around, faster each turn. Click any stage for its anatomy,
sample dialogue, and — color-coded on the border — **who can act there**.

The green exits are the chapter's argument that either person can cut the loop
at any point: *time the demand* and *system carries the reminder* belong to
the family member; *the pause* belongs to the person with ADHD; *repair* and
*call the loop* belong to either. Each exit shows its replacement sentence —
"want me to add it to the board?" instead of "did you do it YET?" — and
cutting an edge is sticky: press **Run one turn** afterward and watch the
turn stop dead at your cut. That's analysis turned into rehearsal.

## Lesson Plan

1. **Watch it spin** (2 min): Run one turn untouched, reading the dialogue.
2. **Anatomy** (4 min): Click all seven stages. Each family identifies the stage their loop usually ignites at.
3. **Cut it** (4 min): Each person cuts the exit *they* own, reads the replacement sentence aloud, and re-runs the turn.
4. **Agree** (3 min): Pick one replacement sentence to actually adopt this week, and put its supporting system (the board, the pause protocol) in place.
