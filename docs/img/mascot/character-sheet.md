# Bhindi — Character Sheet

The canonical description of this book's learning mascot. Anything that
generates new Bhindi art or writes Bhindi dialogue should start here.

## Identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Name | Bhindi |
| Species | Cat — domestic shorthair mix |
| Coat | Soft bluish-grey, paler chest, muzzle and paws |
| Eyes | Large, round, warm yellow |
| Ears | Grey with soft pink inner ears |
| Build | Compact, softly rounded — approachable rather than sleek |
| Art style | Flat vector, clean bold outlines, soft rounded shapes, minimal shading |
| Background | Fully transparent RGBA, no scene, no ground shadow, no text |

## Personality

Bhindi is a **companion, not a teacher**. She sits with the reader rather than
instructing them. She is calm, warm, and steady — the presence in the room
when something is hard.

What she is:

- Gentle and unhurried
- Attentive — she notices, and says so plainly
- Encouraging without cheerleading

What she is **not**:

- Never manic, zany, or wacky
- Never a scold, and never disappointed in the reader
- Never a mascot for hyperactivity as a joke — this book does not make ADHD
  traits into a punchline
- Never a clinician; she has no medical opinions

## Voice

Short sentences. Plain words. First person, present tense. One or two
sentences per appearance — three at the absolute most.

Good:

> "This chapter has the part most people skip. Take it slowly — I'll wait."

> "You did the hard reading. That counts, even if nothing feels different yet."

Bad:

- "Hi there, friends! Let's dive into the wonderful world of executive
  function!" — too bouncy, and *dive into* is on the banned list
- "Remember: you can do anything if you believe in yourself!" — empty
  cheerleading
- "Studies show that 70% of children..." — she doesn't cite research; the
  chapter does that

## Poses

| Pose | File | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Neutral | `neutral.png` | General purpose, sidebars, favicon source |
| Welcome | `welcome.png` | Chapter openings — at most one per chapter |
| Thinking | `thinking.png` | A key concept worth pausing on |
| Tip | `tip.png` | A concrete, actionable hint |
| Warning | `warning.png` | A common misreading — never danger, never scolding |
| Encouraging | `encouraging.png` | Genuinely hard material |
| Celebration | `celebration.png` | Chapter close — at most one per chapter |

## Where Bhindi does not go

This book covers shame, burnout, caregiver strain, grief after a late
diagnosis, and suicide-risk guidance. A cartoon cat next to that material
undercuts it.

**Do not place Bhindi:**

- Anywhere near crisis or self-harm content (Chapter 6's 988 guidance)
- In the grief-after-diagnosis material (Chapter 11)
- In the caregiver strain and burnout sections (Chapters 6 and 15)
- Beside anything about shame or years of negative feedback (Chapter 11)

The `encouraging` pose is the one to reach for near hard-but-not-raw content.
When in doubt, leave the page clean. Silence is a legitimate design choice, and
this book's tone is worth more than mascot coverage.
