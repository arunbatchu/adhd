# ADHD Across The Lifespan

## Summary

This chapter follows ADHD from childhood through adulthood, with special attention to how it shows up in girls and women and why so many are diagnosed late. It covers the transitions — into college, into work, into independent living — where support systems change hands. After this chapter, readers understand how a bright, hard-working person can reach adulthood before anyone names what is going on.
