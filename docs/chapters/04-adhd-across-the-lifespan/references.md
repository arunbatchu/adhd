# References: ADHD Across The Lifespan

1. [Adult attention deficit hyperactivity disorder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adult_attention_deficit_hyperactivity_disorder) - Wikipedia - Persistence of ADHD into adulthood, adult presentation, and late diagnosis — the research backdrop for this chapter's lifespan arc.

2. [Attention deficit hyperactivity disorder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attention_deficit_hyperactivity_disorder) - Wikipedia - Includes epidemiology and sex-difference findings behind the gender diagnosis gap this chapter explains.

3. [Menopause](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Menopause) - Wikipedia - The hormonal transition whose estrogen decline interacts with ADHD compensation systems — background for this chapter's hormones section.

4. Women with Attention Deficit Disorder - Sari Solden - Underwood Books - Solden's 1995 book was the first major work on women's ADHD; she named the shame-and-disorganization spiral and the invisible, internalized presentation that this chapter's girls-and-women section builds on.

5. Understanding Girls with ADHD (Second Edition) - Kathleen Nadeau, Ellen Littman, and Patricia Quinn - Advantage Books - Littman and colleagues documented how girls' presentations differ — internalizing, masking, referral bias — and their framework is the standard account of why the nets miss girls.

6. [For Adults - Overview](https://chadd.org/for-adults/overview/) - CHADD - Adult diagnosis, late diagnosis, and life-stage resources from the national resource center.

7. [ADHD - NIMH](https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/topics/attention-deficit-hyperactivity-disorder-adhd) - National Institute of Mental Health - Federal overview including lifespan course and adult recognition.

8. [ADDA](https://add.org/) - Attention Deficit Disorder Association - The organization dedicated to adults with ADHD: late-diagnosis stories, workplace and life-stage support, and peer community.

9. [ADDitude Magazine](https://www.additudemag.com/) - ADDitude - Extensive clinician-reviewed coverage of women's ADHD, hormones, late diagnosis, and the adult diagnosis journey.

10. [ADHD Evidence Project](https://adhdevidence.org/) - Stephen Faraone et al. - Consensus evidence on persistence into adulthood and underdiagnosis in women — useful ammunition for the skeptical-relative conversations this chapter anticipates.
