# Emotional Life And Identity

## Summary

This chapter addresses the emotional side of ADHD that rarely makes the diagnostic checklists: emotional dysregulation, rejection sensitivity, shame from years of negative feedback, and the grief and relief of a late diagnosis. It builds toward self-advocacy — being able to explain your own ADHD and ask for what you need. After this chapter, both readers understand that the emotional weight is part of ADHD, not a character flaw.
