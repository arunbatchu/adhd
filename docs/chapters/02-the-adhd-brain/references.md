# References: The ADHD Brain

1. [Executive functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_functions) - Wikipedia - The cognitive-science treatment of the skill set this chapter organizes around: working memory, inhibition, flexibility, and their development and measurement.

2. [Prefrontal cortex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prefrontal_cortex) - Wikipedia - Anatomy and function of the brain's executive suite, including its long maturation timeline — the biology behind this chapter's construction-schedule story.

3. [Reward system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reward_system) - Wikipedia - The dopamine-driven circuits of wanting, motivation, and reinforcement that this chapter's broken-discount-rate economist runs on.

4. ADHD and the Nature of Self-Control - Russell A. Barkley - Guilford Press - Barkley originated the influential theory reframing ADHD as a disorder of self-regulation and executive function rather than attention — the intellectual foundation of this chapter's "steering, not shortage" model, including his concept of time blindness.

5. A New Understanding of ADHD in Children and Adults: Executive Function Impairments - Thomas E. Brown - Routledge - Brown's orchestra-conductor analogy for executive function — the musicians are skilled; the conductor cues them — is the most widely taught picture of why ability and performance separate in ADHD.

6. [Russell Barkley Fact Sheets](https://www.russellbarkley.org/factsheets.html) - russellbarkley.org - The researcher's own concise fact sheets on executive function, the 30% developmental delay rule of thumb, and evidence-based management.

7. [ADHD - NIMH](https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/topics/attention-deficit-hyperactivity-disorder-adhd) - National Institute of Mental Health - Federal research overview including the neuroscience and brain-development findings this chapter summarizes.

8. [ADHD - APA Topics](https://www.apa.org/topics/adhd) - American Psychological Association - The APA's evidence summaries on ADHD's cognitive and behavioral science, written for non-specialists.

9. [How to ADHD](https://howtoadhd.com/) - Jessica McCabe - The companion site to the popular channel: brain-science explainers ("brain hacks" grounded in executive-function research) in the friendliest format available for teens and adults.

10. [ADHD and Sleep](https://www.sleepfoundation.org/mental-health/adhd-and-sleep) - Sleep Foundation - Clinician-reviewed explainer on the two-directional ADHD-sleep relationship this chapter introduces, including delayed sleep phase.
