# References: Work And Career

1. [Americans with Disabilities Act of 1990](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Americans_with_Disabilities_Act_of_1990) - Wikipedia - The US law behind workplace accommodation rights: coverage, reasonable accommodation, and the interactive process.

2. [Reasonable accommodation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reasonable_accommodation) - Wikipedia - The legal concept this chapter's accommodation menu operationalizes, including undue-hardship limits.

3. [Telecommuting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telecommuting) - Wikipedia - Research on remote work's effects — context for this chapter's honest both-edges treatment of remote work with ADHD.

4. The ADHD Guide to Career Success (Second Edition) - Kathleen G. Nadeau - Routledge - Nadeau originated the career-fit-first approach for ADHD: matching job shape to brain wiring before fighting symptoms, the exact inversion this chapter teaches.

5. More Attention, Less Deficit - Ari Tuckman - Specialty Press - Tuckman's integrative adult-ADHD handbook is known for its workplace chapters: honest trade-off framing on disclosure and accommodations that this chapter's graduated ladder builds on.

6. [ADHD Accommodations - JAN](https://askjan.org/disabilities/Attention-Deficit-Hyperactivity-Disorder-AD-HD.cfm) - Job Accommodation Network - The US Department of Labor-funded service's ADHD page: concrete accommodation ideas and the legal process, employer-tested.

7. [Mental Health Conditions in the Workplace - EEOC](https://www.eeoc.gov/laws/guidance/depression-ptsd-other-mental-health-conditions-workplace-your-legal-rights) - US Equal Employment Opportunity Commission - Official guidance on workplace rights for mental-health conditions, including disclosure and accommodation requests.

8. [ADDA](https://add.org/) - Attention Deficit Disorder Association - Workplace webinars, career peer groups, and adult-ADHD professional resources.

9. [For Adults - CHADD](https://chadd.org/for-adults/overview/) - CHADD - Career and workplace strategy resources from the national resource center.

10. [ADDitude Magazine](https://www.additudemag.com/) - ADDitude - Clinician-reviewed workplace coverage: disclosure decisions, accommodation scripts, and career-fit stories.
