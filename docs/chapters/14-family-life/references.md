# References: Family Life And Communication

1. [Family therapy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Family_therapy) - Wikipedia - The systems view of families — behavior shaped by the whole system — that this chapter's roles-and-drift analysis comes from.

2. [Parent management training](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parent_management_training) - Wikipedia - The evidence tradition behind the household-redesign techniques this chapter applies: clear instructions, reinforcement, and repaired interaction spirals.

3. [Oppositional defiant disorder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oppositional_defiant_disorder) - Wikipedia - Background on the defiance pattern whose overlap with transition-tax behavior this chapter untangles.

4. 12 Principles for Raising a Child with ADHD - Russell A. Barkley - Guilford Press - Barkley distilled decades of parent-training research into principles — prioritize the relationship, use incentives before punishment, practice forgiveness — that anticipate this chapter's talk-to-the-mechanism house rules.

5. The Explosive Child - Ross W. Greene - Harper - Greene's "kids do well if they can" reframe and collaborative problem-solving method changed how families read defiance: as lagging skills meeting unsolved problems, the same lens this chapter applies to the conflict cycle.

6. [For Parents - Overview](https://chadd.org/for-parents/overview/) - CHADD - Parenting resources, parent-training program listings, and family-communication guidance from the national resource center.

7. [ADHD Resource Center](https://www.aacap.org/AACAP/Families_and_Youth/Resource_Centers/ADHD_Resource_Center/Home.aspx) - American Academy of Child and Adolescent Psychiatry - Professional-body family guides on ADHD's household impact and evidence-based parenting approaches.

8. [Understood.org](https://www.understood.org/) - Understood - Family-communication and homework-system resources for raising kids who learn and think differently.

9. [ADDitude Magazine](https://www.additudemag.com/) - ADDitude - Extensive family-life coverage: nagging cycles, chore systems, and supporting adult children — this chapter's territory in article form.

10. [How to ADHD](https://howtoadhd.com/) - Jessica McCabe - Family-facing explainers that help relatives understand the mechanism before reaching for character verdicts.
