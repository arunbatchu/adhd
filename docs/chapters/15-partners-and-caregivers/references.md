# References: Partners And Caring For The Caregiver

1. [Couples therapy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Couples_therapy) - Wikipedia - The therapy tradition behind this chapter's counseling section, including approaches and evidence.

2. [Caregiver burden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caregiver_burden) - Wikipedia - The research construct of caregiver strain — measurement, health effects, and interventions — that this chapter treats as a first-class clinical topic.

3. [Respite care](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Respite_care) - Wikipedia - What respite is and why scheduled, genuinely off-duty relief protects caregivers.

4. The ADHD Effect on Marriage - Melissa Orlov - Specialty Press - Orlov named and mapped the parent-child dynamic in ADHD-affected marriages — this chapter's partner-parent trap — and built the repair sequence couples counseling for ADHD now standardly uses.

5. ADHD After Dark - Ari Tuckman - Routledge - Tuckman's survey research on intimacy in ADHD-affected couples brought the bedroom effects of the trap into the open, grounding this chapter's shared-engineering approach to desire and fairness.

6. [ADHD and Marriage](https://www.additudemag.com/) - ADDitude - Clinician-reviewed relationship coverage, including Orlov's and Tuckman's own articles on the couple dynamics this chapter describes.

7. [Support For Caregivers - CHADD](https://chadd.org/for-parents/overview/) - CHADD - Parent-and-caregiver programs and support communities, including caregiver-specific training.

8. [ADDA](https://add.org/) - Attention Deficit Disorder Association - Adult and partner support groups — the room where the caregiver's specific loneliness meets people who fully get it.

9. [Support group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Support_group) - Wikipedia - The evidence and formats for peer support, background for this chapter's community recommendations.

10. [988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline](https://988lifeline.org/) - SAMHSA - The US crisis line: caregiver chapters carry it too, because supporters in crisis deserve immediate help — call or text 988.
