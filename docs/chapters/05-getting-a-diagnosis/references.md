# References: Getting A Diagnosis

1. [DSM-5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DSM-5) - Wikipedia - The diagnostic manual behind every evaluation this chapter describes: history, structure, and the revision process that shaped ADHD's criteria.

2. [Psychological evaluation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychological_evaluation) - Wikipedia - What formal psychological assessment involves — interviews, standardized instruments, collateral data — matching this chapter's anatomy of an evaluation.

3. [Attention deficit hyperactivity disorder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attention_deficit_hyperactivity_disorder) - Wikipedia - Includes the diagnosis section covering criteria, differential diagnosis, and assessment debates this chapter turns into consumer skills.

4. Taking Charge of Adult ADHD (Second Edition) - Russell A. Barkley - Guilford Press - Barkley's step-by-step walkthrough of what a legitimate adult evaluation contains — and his insistence on collateral informants and childhood evidence — set the template this chapter's evaluation anatomy follows.

5. Driven to Distraction (Revised Edition) - Edward M. Hallowell and John J. Ratey - Anchor Books - Hallowell and Ratey popularized diagnosis-as-explanation: their case-story method showed a generation of readers what recognition feels like and why a name changes a life story, anticipating this chapter's "what a diagnosis means."

6. [Diagnosis of ADHD - NIMH](https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/topics/attention-deficit-hyperactivity-disorder-adhd) - National Institute of Mental Health - Federal guidance on how ADHD is identified in children and adults and who can evaluate.

7. [About ADHD - Overview](https://chadd.org/about-adhd/overview/) - CHADD - Includes evaluation guidance and clinician-finding resources from the national resource center.

8. [NICE Guideline NG87](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng87) - National Institute for Health and Care Excellence (UK) - The UK's evidence-based clinical guideline for ADHD diagnosis and management — a rigorous non-US benchmark for what thorough practice looks like.

9. [CADDRA](https://caddra.ca/) - Canadian ADHD Resource Alliance - Canadian clinical practice guidelines and assessment toolkits, including the rating scales evaluators actually use.

10. [ADHD Resource Center](https://www.aacap.org/AACAP/Families_and_Youth/Resource_Centers/ADHD_Resource_Center/Home.aspx) - American Academy of Child and Adolescent Psychiatry - Professional-body FAQs on evaluation, diagnosis, and finding qualified clinicians for children and teens.
