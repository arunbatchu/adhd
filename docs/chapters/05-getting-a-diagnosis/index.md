# Getting A Diagnosis

## Summary

This chapter walks through the diagnostic process end to end: who can diagnose, what an evaluation involves, how to prepare, and what the report does and does not mean. It also covers the pitfalls — misdiagnosis, underdiagnosis, cost, and finding the right clinician. After this chapter, readers can navigate an evaluation as informed participants rather than anxious passengers.
