# Co-occurring Conditions

## Summary

This chapter covers the conditions that commonly travel with ADHD — anxiety, depression, learning disabilities, autism, sleep disorders — plus ADHD burnout. It explains how clinicians decide what to treat first when several things are true at once. After this chapter, readers can recognize when something beyond ADHD needs its own attention.
