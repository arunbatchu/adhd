# References: School And College

1. [Individualized Education Program](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Individualized_Education_Program) - Wikipedia - The US special-education vehicle: legal basis, team process, and contents, matching this chapter's IEP section.

2. [Section 504 of the Rehabilitation Act](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Section_504_of_the_Rehabilitation_Act) - Wikipedia - The civil-rights law behind 504 plans and post-secondary access, with history and scope.

3. [Spaced repetition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spaced_repetition) - Wikipedia - The memory research behind this chapter's study-technique recommendations, including the scheduling algorithms study apps use.

4. Teaching Teens with ADD, ADHD & Executive Function Deficits (Second Edition) - Chris A. Zeigler Dendy - Woodbine House - Dendy pioneered the executive-function-first school playbook: her teacher-facing summaries and accommodation menus are the practical template behind this chapter's mechanism-linked request lists.

5. Smart but Scattered Teens - Richard Guare, Peg Dawson, and Colin Guare - Guilford Press - The Dawson-Guare executive-skills framework applied to adolescents — the transfer-the-systems-before-the-cliff approach this chapter's handoff audit operationalizes.

6. [The Difference Between IEPs and 504 Plans](https://www.understood.org/en/articles/the-difference-between-ieps-and-504-plans) - Understood.org - The clearest consumer comparison of the two vehicles, with examples — a companion to this chapter's table.

7. [Understood.org](https://www.understood.org/) - Understood - Extensive school-support resources: accommodation lists, meeting scripts, and parent rights across learning and attention differences.

8. [For Parents - Overview](https://chadd.org/for-parents/overview/) - CHADD - The national resource center's education pages, including school collaboration and accommodation guidance.

9. [AHEAD](https://www.ahead.org/) - Association on Higher Education and Disability - The professional association for college disability services — useful for understanding how the offices this chapter describes actually operate.

10. [Special education in the United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_education_in_the_United_States) - Wikipedia - Broader legal and historical context for IDEA and the rights framework school accommodations sit inside.
