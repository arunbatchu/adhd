# References: Presentations And Everyday Traits

1. [Hyperfocus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyperfocus) - Wikipedia - The research and clinical picture of intense absorption states, including their double-edged role in ADHD that this chapter describes.

2. [Adult attention deficit hyperactivity disorder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adult_attention_deficit_hyperactivity_disorder) - Wikipedia - How ADHD presents past childhood — internal restlessness, disorganization, the costume changes this chapter's traits catalog documents.

3. [DSM-5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DSM-5) - Wikipedia - Background on the diagnostic manual whose eighteen symptoms and counting rules this chapter's presentation section operates.

4. Taking Charge of Adult ADHD (Second Edition) - Russell A. Barkley - Guilford Press - Barkley coined and developed "time blindness" and the collapsed now/not-now time horizon — the single most-used explanation of ADHD lateness and deadline behavior, taught directly in this chapter.

5. Attention Deficit Disorder: A Different Perception - Thom Hartmann - Healing Arts Press - Hartmann's hunter-in-a-farmer's-world analogy is the most famous reframe of ADHD traits as context-dependent — scanning, novelty-seeking, and quick reaction as assets in one environment and impairments in another.

6. [For Adults - Overview](https://chadd.org/for-adults/overview/) - CHADD - The national resource center's adult-focused overview: recognizing inattentive and internalized presentations that childhood stereotypes miss.

7. [ADDitude: ADHD/ADD Basics](https://www.additudemag.com/category/adhd-add/) - ADDitude - Clinician-reviewed articles on everyday traits — time blindness, hyperfocus, masking — with lived-experience detail that complements the clinical picture.

8. [ADDA](https://add.org/) - Attention Deficit Disorder Association - The adult-ADHD organization: peer support, workplace resources, and adult-presentation education aligned with this chapter's grown-up trait catalog.

9. [ADHD - MedlinePlus](https://medlineplus.gov/attentiondeficithyperactivitydisorder.html) - US National Library of Medicine - Vetted plain-language resources on symptoms and presentations across ages.

10. [ADHD Resource Center](https://www.aacap.org/AACAP/Families_and_Youth/Resource_Centers/ADHD_Resource_Center/Home.aspx) - American Academy of Child and Adolescent Psychiatry - Clinical FAQs and family guides on how ADHD presents in children and teens, from the professional body for child psychiatry.
